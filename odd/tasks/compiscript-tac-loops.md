- **S2 — Execution and labels.** While checks its condition before each iteration; do-while executes its body once before checking; for executes initializer once, checks its optional condition before each iteration, executes its optional post after the body, and repeats. Omitted for condition means true. Break targets the nearest loop's exit and skips the for post; continue targets while's condition, do-while's condition, or for's post, respectively. Nested loops and conditional transfers must not cross loop boundaries. Existing short circuit and branch static validation remain in force.

Respect that initializer contexts consume their own semicolon. This intentional correction changes prior AST/analysis reports for omitted-condition/present-post headers; all other protected existing reports must remain identical. Do not change grammar/generated files, AST schema, semantic policy or dependencies.
